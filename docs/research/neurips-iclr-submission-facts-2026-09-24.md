## 1. NeurIPS

### 1a. Submission deadline pattern

| Cycle | Abstract deadline | Full paper deadline | Supplementary deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NeurIPS 2025** | May 11, 2025 AoE | May 15, 2025 AoE | May 22, 2025 AoE |
| **NeurIPS 2026** | May 4, 2026 AOE | May 6, 2026 AOE | same deadline as full paper |

**VERIFIED.** NeurIPS 2025: "**Abstract submission deadline:** May 11, 2025 AoE", "**Full paper submission deadline:** May 15, 2025 AOE", "**Technical appendices and supplemental materials deadline:** May 22, 2025 AoE" ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers)); corroborated by the dates table ([NeurIPS 2025 Dates](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/Dates)). NeurIPS 2026: "Abstract submission deadline: May 4, 2026 (AOE)", "Full paper submission deadline, including all supplementary materials: May 6, 2026 AOE" ([NeurIPS 2026 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers)); corroborated by ([NeurIPS 2026 Dates](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/Dates)).

**Pattern:** deadlines consistently fall in the **first half of May**, AoE.

### 1d. Page limit — main submission

- **9 content pages**, including all figures and tables. Additional pages for references, optional technical appendices, and the mandatory paper checklist **do not count**. Accepted papers get **+1 content page** for camera-ready (i.e. 10). Max file size **50 MB**. Word template discontinued.
  - NeurIPS 2025: "The main text of a submitted paper is limited to **nine content pages**, including all figures and tables. Additional pages containing references, checklist, and the optional technical appendices do not count as content pages. If your submission is accepted, you will be allowed an additional content page for the camera-ready version. The maximum file size for a main paper submission is 50MB." ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers))
  - NeurIPS 2026: identical 9-page limit and +1 camera-ready page; "The maximum file size is 50MB"; "This is the **only** template we will accept (note: Microsoft Word template has been discontinued)." ([NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook))
- **References: unlimited.** "There is no page limit for the technical appendices" ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers)); references and appendices are explicitly excluded from the content-page count in both cycles.
- **Checklist is mandatory and does not count toward the page limit.** "Do not remove the checklist: **The papers not including the checklist will be desk rejected**." ([NeurIPS Paper Checklist Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist))
- **Supplementary/code: up to 100 MB** in a single anonymized ZIP ([NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook)); 100 MB also stated for 2025 ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers)).
- **Style file:** NeurIPS LaTeX style; "Submissions that violate the NeurIPS style (e.g., by decreasing margins or font sizes) or page limits may be desk rejected." ([NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook))

This policy applies to any supplementary or linked material as well, including code." Violations → desk rejection. ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers), [NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook))
- **Exception — Datasets & Benchmarks track was optional single/double-blind in 2025: VERIFIED.** "**Authors can choose to submit either single-blind or double-blind**." ([NeurIPS 2025 Datasets & Benchmarks CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForDatasetsBenchmarks)) For 2026 the track was renamed **Evaluations & Datasets (ED) Track** ([NeurIPS blog, 2026-03-23](https://blog.neurips.cc/2026/03/23/introducing-the-evaluations-datasets-track-at-neurips-2026/)); its 2026 blind policy text was not fetched → 待核实.
- **arXiv preprints allowed — VERIFIED.** "The existence of non-anonymous preprints (on arXiv or other online repositories, personal websites, social media) will not result in rejection." Caveats: use the template's "preprint" option, don't write "Under review at NeurIPS", and "aggressive advertising of papers under submission may be deemed a violation." ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers), [NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook))

### 1f. Real-robot / physical hardware experiments required?

**待核实 — no official CFP or handbook language requires or expects physical hardware experiments.** Neither CFP imposes an experimental-substrate requirement.

Closest official language (does *not* establish a requirement, but shows hardware setups are anticipated): the code-submission guidelines accept non-self-contained code when "Specialized hardware is required to run the experiment (e.g., specialized accelerators or **robotic platforms**)." ### 1g. Tolerance for "system integration" contributions — official language

NeurIPS is the more explicit of the two venues here, and 2026 added a formal contribution-type mechanism.

**NeurIPS 2026 — contribution types are a required author choice (VERIFIED):**
> "**General:** We expect that most submissions will fall into this type. **Theory:** The main contribution is via theoretical analyses and proofs. **Use-Inspired:** The main contribution is in framing or designing approaches to meet the needs of a specific real-world application. (This often involves, e.g., engaging with domain experts.). **Concept & Feasibility:** The main contribution is a highly novel, high potential reward idea with scope beyond what can be validated in a single paper. **Negative Results:** The main contribution is in understanding a negative result."
([NeurIPS 2026 Reviewer Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines); same list in [Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook), announced in [NeurIPS blog, 2026-04-16](https://blog.neurips.cc/2026/04/16/a-choice-of-contribution-types-at-neurips-2026/))

> "It is not possible for Contribution Type to be changed after submission, either by the authors or by the reviewers." ([NeurIPS 2026 Reviewer Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines))

**Explicit statement that a new algorithm is NOT required (VERIFIED) — this is the strongest official support for a systems/integration paper:**
> "**Originality** — Does the work provide new insights, deepen understanding, or highlight important properties of existing methods? ... As the questions above indicates, **originality does not necessarily require introducing an entirely new method.** Rather, a work that provides novel insights by evaluating existing methods, or demonstrates improved efficiency, fairness, etc. is also equally valuable."
([NeurIPS 2026 Reviewer Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines))

**Use-Inspired type explicitly tolerates integrating existing methods (VERIFIED):**
> "**Originality need not mean wholly novel methods.** It could mean a novel combination of existing methods to match the characteristics of the data, or a novel way of framing a stereotypical ML problem due to the particular needs of the use case." — with the caveat: "**Papers should provide insights to guide methods development.** Thus, a paper may apply existing methods in a new context, but in such cases the discussion should analyze *why* certain methodological choices are effective or ineffective."
([NeurIPS 2026 Reviewer Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines))

**Systems/infrastructure is a named in-scope topic (VERIFIED):**
- NeurIPS 2025 topic list includes "**Infrastructure (e.g., libraries, improved implementation and scalability, distributed solutions)**" ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers))
- NeurIPS 2026 topic list includes "**Robotics, embodied systems, and engineering**" and "**SysML Infrastructure**" ([NeurIPS 2026 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers))

**Datasets/benchmarks/tooling route (VERIFIED):** the D&B → **Evaluations & Datasets** track "welcomes all work on *data-centric machine learning research (DMLR)* that enable or accelerate ML research, covering ML datasets and benchmarks as well as algorithms, tools, methods, and analyses for working with ML data" and is "proud to support the open source movement by *encouraging submissions of open-source libraries and tools* that enable or accelerate ML research." ([NeurIPS 2025 Datasets & Benchmarks CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForDatasetsBenchmarks))

**Paper checklist is mandatory** ([NeurIPS Paper Checklist Guidelines](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist)).

### 1h. Author response / rebuttal + supplementary & code policy

**NeurIPS 2025 — VERIFIED dates:** "Author Rebuttal Begins Jul 24 '25" → "Author Rebuttal Ends Jul 30 '25"; "Reviewer Author Discussions Begins Jul 31 '25" → "Ends Aug 06 '25". Prose: "Authors will have one week to view and respond to initial reviews. Author responses may not contain any identifying information ... Authors may not submit revisions of their paper or supplemental material." ([NeurIPS 2025 Dates](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/Dates), [NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers))

**NeurIPS 2026 — VERIFIED dates and mechanics:** three phases —
1. "Authors view reviews, meta-reviews and create an initial response."
2. "Author, reviewer, AC discussion."
3. "Reviewer, AC discussion."
"The per-review rebuttal limit is **10,000 characters**. You may use plain text with markdown formatting supported by OpenReview, but **you cannot upload any additional files**." "**Do not use links in any part of the response.** The only exception is if the reviewers asked for code, in which case you can send an anonymized link to the AC in an Official Comment." Dates: reviews released Jul 22, 2026; author+reviewer+AC discussion Jul 27–Aug 3, 2026; reviewer+AC discussion Aug 3–10, 2026. ([NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook), [NeurIPS 2026 Dates](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/Dates))

**Supplementary/code policy — VERIFIED:** supplementary is a single anonymized ZIP up to 100 MB; code upload is "encouraged … in order to help reviewers assess the quality of the work"; reviewers must keep it confidential; accepted papers must provide de-anonymized reproducibility links. Code guidelines note the robotic-platform exception quoted in 1f. Camera-ready must include a **funding statement and competing-interests disclosure**. ([NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook))

The full paper submission deadline is **September 24, 2025 AOE**." ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide), [ICLR 2026 CFP](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers), [ICLR 2026 Dates](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/Dates)) ICLR 2027: "**Abstract deadline** Sep 18, 2026 AOE", "**Paper deadline** Sep 25, 2026 11:59 PM AOE". ⚠️ **Official-page inconsistency, reported as-is:** the ICLR 2027 CFP and Author Guidelines render the paper deadline as "Sep 25, 2026 11:59 PM AOE **or**" — a dangling, truncated sentence. Treat Nov 5 (reviews) / Dec 16 (decisions) as the anchor and Sep 25 as the operative deadline, but be aware the site contradicts itself.

### 2d. Page limit — main submission

- **9 pages of main text at submission → 10 pages during discussion/rebuttal and camera-ready.**
  - ICLR 2026: "At the time of submission, the main text should be **9 pages or fewer**. During the discussion/rebuttal phase and for the camera ready, the page limit will be increased to **10 pages** ... This limit will be strictly enforced. Papers with main text beyond the page limit will be desk-rejected." ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide))
  - ICLR 2027: identical wording ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines))
- **References: unlimited.** "The list of references does not count towards the page limit, and **unlimited additional pages are allowed for the bibliography/references**." ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide), [ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines))
- **Appendix: unlimited but not guaranteed to be read.** "Authors may use as many pages of appendices (after the bibliography) as they wish, but reviewers are not required to read the appendix." (same URLs)
- **Also not counted toward the limit:** optional ethics statement (≤1 page), optional reproducibility statement, acknowledgements, and (new in 2027) the required AI use statement. ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide), [ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines))
- **Style files (now hosted on the venue domain, not GitHub):** ICLR 2027 — `https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip` ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines)); ICLR 2026 used the ICLR Master-Template zip ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide)). Both are the ICLR LaTeX style, not the NeurIPS style file.
- **No separate supplementary deadline:** "The deadline is the same for the full paper and for the supplementary materials." ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines))

### 2e. Blind policy + arXiv during review

- **Double-blind — VERIFIED, mandatory.** "ICLR 2027 is double blind ... Any paper where author identity is revealed in either the main text or the supplementary material will be desk rejected. Note that related arxiv papers by the same authors do not break anonymity; if cited, these should be cited in third person." ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines); same for 2026 at [ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide))
- **arXiv during review explicitly allowed — VERIFIED.** "**Submission of the paper to archival repositories such as arXiv is allowed during the review period.**" ICLR 2027 adds: "Submission of the paper to archival repositories such as arXiv is allowed during the review period." ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide), [ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines))
- ⚠️ **Asymmetry worth flagging:** ICLR submissions are **archival and non-deletable**. "after the paper submission deadline, if an author chooses to withdraw a submission, it will remain hosted by OpenReview in a publicly visible 'withdrawn papers' section ... **Withdrawn papers will be de-anonymized immediately.**" ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines)) NeurIPS rejected papers remain non-public unless the authors opt in ([NeurIPS 2025 CFP](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers)).
- **New for ICLR 2027 — mandatory AI use statement.** "we require authors to disclose AI use in a **mandatory section** in their paper (this section will not be counted towards the page limit)." A task list defines what is *required* to disclose (e.g. "implement methods", "design or provide feedback on research methodology or experiments", "assist in the writing of proofs") vs *recommended* (e.g. "create or edit software code", "draft parts of a research paper"). ([ICLR 2027 AI Policy for Authors](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors))

**待核实 — no official CFP, author guide, or reviewer guide requires or expects physical hardware experiments.** Robotics is listed only as an in-scope *application area*, not as a requirement: "applications to robotics, autonomy, planning" appears in the subject-area list of both cycles ([ICLR 2026 CFP](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers), [ICLR 2027 CFP](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers)).

### 2g. Tolerance for "system integration" contributions — official language

**No explicit "no novelty requirement" statement exists in the ICLR 2027/2026 CFP, author guide, or reviewer guide → 待核实 for that specific phrasing.** What ICLR *does* publish, verbatim:

**Reviewer guidance against leaderboard/SOTA tunnel vision (ICLR 2027 — VERIFIED):**
> "**Be open-minded**: not all work needs to compete on an established leaderboard, or work in an established theoretical framework—instead, ask yourself whether the results are likely to be interesting or surprising to a broad audience, or if it is possible they will take the field in new directions."
([ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines))

**SOTA is not required (both cycles — VERIFIED):**
> "If a submission does not achieve state-of-the-art results, is that grounds for rejection? No, a lack of state-of-the-art results does not by itself constitute grounds for rejection. Submissions bring value to the ICLR community when they convincingly demonstrate new, relevant, impactful knowledge."
([ICLR 2026 Reviewer Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuide), [ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines))

**What counts as "significance" (ICLR 2027 — VERIFIED):** "Does it contribute new knowledge and sufficient value to the community? Note, this does not necessarily require state-of-the-art results. Submissions bring value to the ICLR community when they convincingly demonstrate new, relevant, impactful knowledge (incl., empirical, theoretical, **for practitioners**, etc)." ([ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines))

**Systems/tooling is an explicit in-scope topic (VERIFIED):** both cycles list "**infrastructure, software libraries, hardware, etc.**" and "**neurosymbolic & hybrid AI systems**" among relevant topics ([ICLR 2026 CFP](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers), [ICLR 2027 CFP](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers)).

**Contribution-type framing:** ICLR has **no** contribution-type taxonomy comparable to NeurIPS 2026's five types. The only positioning axes offered are the subject-area list and the reviewer's four questions ("What is the specific question and/or problem tackled by the paper? Is the approach well motivated, including being well-placed in the literature? Does the paper support the claims? What is the significance of the work?"). ([ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines))

**Net read (interpretation, not a quote):** for a system-integration paper, ICLR's official text is *permissive about method novelty* ("originality does not necessarily require…" is NeurIPS wording; ICLR's equivalent is "not all work needs to compete on an established leaderboard" plus "for practitioners") but places the burden on demonstrating **insight and significance**, and ICLR 2027's reciprocal-review quota makes an unqualified-author submission high-risk (see 2h/§3).

### 2h. Author response / rebuttal + supplementary & code policy

**ICLR 2026 — VERIFIED:** reviews released Nov 11, 2025; "**Nov 11 – December 3: Public discussion period.** Once reviews have been posted, authors can post responses to the reviews as comments on OpenReview. Authors can also **revise their paper until the end of the public discussion period** (Dec 3). If such a revision is made, a **pdfdiff** will be applied." Then "**December 3 – Jan 22: Private discussion among the reviewers and ACs.**" ([ICLR 2026 Author Guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide))

**ICLR 2027 — VERIFIED:** "Nov 05 – Nov 18: Public discussion period" (authors may revise; pdfdiff applied); "Nov 19 – Dec 16: Private discussion among the reviewers and ACs"; "Dec 16: Decision notification." ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines)) Reviewer-side tasks: reviews written Oct 1–21; recommendations due Nov 25; borderline meetings Nov 26–Dec 2 ([ICLR 2027 Reviewer Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines)).

**Key structural difference from NeurIPS:** ICLR permits **revising the submitted PDF during the public discussion period**; NeurIPS 2026 explicitly does not — "authors may not submit revisions of their paper or supplemental material, but may post their responses as a discussion in OpenReview." ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines), [NeurIPS 2026 Main Track Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook))

**Supplementary/code policy — VERIFIED:** single file recommended (paper + supplementary text after references); "We encourage all authors to submit code as part of their submission. Note that reviewers are **encouraged, but not required** to review supplementary material." Three sanctioned ways to share code anonymously: (1) anonymized .zip in supplementary, (2) anonymous repository linked in the paper, (3) anonymous repo link posted as a comment to reviewers/ACs after forums open. Demo/video links allowed if fully anonymous and non-tracking. ([ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines))

## 3. ICLR 2027 policy changes that materially affect eligibility

All VERIFIED on [ICLR 2027 Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines) and [ICLR blog, 2026-09-02, Submission policies for ICLR 2027](https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/09/02/submission-policies-for-iclr-2027/):

- **20-paper cap:** "No author may appear as a co-author on more than 20 papers."
- **1-paper cap for unqualified authors:** "Each author may appear as a co-author on at most one paper in which no author is an eligible reciprocal reviewer." In plain language: "if you, and all of your co-authors, have never had a paper accepted for publication in a major machine learning conference or journal, you may submit at most one paper to ICLR this year."
- **Qualifying-venue list (subset):** ICLR/NeurIPS/ICML/UAI/AISTATS/JMLR/TMLR; ACL/EMNLP/EACL/NAACL/IJCNLP-AACL/CL/TACL; COLM; CVPR/ICCV/ECCV/PAMI/3DV; AAAI/IJCAI/JAIR; **ICRA/IROS/RSS/CoRL**; KDD; COLT. "only primary conference papers satisfy the requirement; other publications ... (including position papers, 'tiny papers', blog posts, system demonstrations, industry papers, and workshop papers) do not."
  → **Directly relevant to a robotics lab:** ICRA/IROS/RSS/CoRL acceptances *do* qualify a reciprocal reviewer.
- **Enforcement:** "we will randomly desk-reject excess submissions until every author is under quota." Eligibility "determined by papers accepted for publication by the abstract deadline."
- **Reciprocal reviewing:** authors on ≥3 papers must review ≥6 papers; every submission needs ≥1 author registered to review ≥3 papers.
